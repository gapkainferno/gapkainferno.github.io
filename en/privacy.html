Privacy Policy

Last updated: February 2026

1. What data we collect

When you place an order, we collect the following information:

  • First name, last name and patronymic where required for delivery
  • Phone number
  • City and delivery address
  • Email address
  • Order comment, if you choose to add one

2. How we use your data

Your personal data is used only for:

  • Processing and fulfilling your order
  • Contacting you about your order
  • Shipping goods to the address you provided
  • Marketing messages and stock notifications, only with your consent
  • Website traffic analytics through Google Analytics

3. How we store your data

Your data is stored:

  • In a protected database through Google Apps Script services
  • Temporarily in your browser through LocalStorage to make repeat checkout easier
  • We do not transfer your data to third parties without your consent, except when it is necessary to deliver the order, such as sharing delivery details with Nova Poshta, Ukrposhta or another logistics company.

4. Cookies and LocalStorage

Our website uses LocalStorage to:

  • Save your shopping cart
  • Remember checkout details for faster future orders

You can clear this data at any time in your browser settings.

5. Your rights

You have the right to:

  • Request information about stored data
  • Request deletion of your personal data
  • Opt out of receiving messages

6. Security

We take necessary measures to protect your personal data from unauthorized access, modification or destruction.

7. Policy changes

We reserve the right to update this privacy policy. Any changes will be published on this page.

8. Contacts

If you have questions about this privacy policy, contact us:

  • Email: homestead.inferno@gmail.com
