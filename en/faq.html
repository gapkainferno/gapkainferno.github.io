🌱 All about seeds

+ Where does your seed come from?

100% homegrown. All seed shown in the catalog is hand-harvested at our Gapka Homestead Inferno farm. We don't resell third-party seed — we manage the whole process from sprout to harvest.

+ Is your seed isolated?

We work in two directions:

  • Isolated seed: We use physical isolation of flowers for some lines to guarantee varietal purity.
  • Open pollination: For other lines we allow open pollination to preserve strong phenotypes. Each product description clearly states the isolation status.
+ What is germination rate and how do you test it?

We sell only fresh seed (current or previous season) to ensure strong vigor. We perform germination tests using the paper towel method. Expected germination for superhots is ~80-90% when kept at recommended temperatures (+26-30°C).

🔥 INFERNO Sauces

+ What are the ingredients of your sauces and where do they come from?

Each sauce base is made from peppers, tomatoes and vegetables grown on our farm. We control every step from seed planting to labeling. We don't use artificial preservatives — only natural ingredients, vinegar and salt.

+ Are your sauces fermented?

At this stage we specialize in fresh-batch sauces without long fermentation. This preserves the bright, fresh flavor of superhots and the aroma of garden vegetables.

+ How long does an opened bottle keep?

Thanks to natural preservatives an opened bottle can keep in the fridge for up to 6 months, though it's usually consumed much faster!

📦 Orders & Warranty

+ What if the seed doesn't germinate?

Growing superhots involves skill and a bit of natural luck. If you followed instructions (temperature, humidity, light) but had no success — contact us. We review every case individually: offering advice or replacement in the next order. Your harvest is our reputation.

+ Do you ship cash-on-delivery?

Yes. We understand trust is important. You can choose cash-on-delivery with Nova Poshta to inspect packaging before payment.

Other questions

+ Why do you use PET bottles instead of glass?

This is our conscious choice for your convenience. A PET bottle will not break during transport or accidental drops. It also reduces parcel weight and lowers delivery costs. We use high-quality food-grade plastic that does not affect sauce flavor.

+ Should I shake the sauce before using it?

Absolutely. Because we do not use artificial stabilizers or thickeners, natural separation is a sign of a real, natural product. Just shake the bottle to bring the sauce back to its ideal consistency.

+ Are your tomatoes hot too, or are they regular vegetables?

Our tomatoes are not hot. They are selected, flavorful varieties we grow for sauce balance and for our own table. These are sweet, meaty and aromatic tomatoes chosen through years of experiments. If you want the taste of a real homestead tomato, this is it.

+ Where exactly are these products grown?

Our homestead is located in Dnipropetrovsk Oblast, Ukraine. This is real Ukrainian steppe with hot summers, ideal for ripening extreme peppers. When you buy from us, you support a local small farm.
