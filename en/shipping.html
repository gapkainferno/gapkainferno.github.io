Shipping & Payment

💳 Payment

  • Prepayment (QR code / envelope / IBAN): The most convenient option. Please note that we start picking and preparing your seeds or sauces for dispatch only after payment is actually confirmed.
  • Cash on delivery: Pay when you receive the parcel at the post office. Available for Nova Poshta and Ukrposhta.

📦 Shipping

  • Nova Poshta: Delivery to a branch, parcel locker or by courier. Orders are dispatched within 1-3 business days.
  • Ukrposhta: Delivery to any branch in Ukraine, standard or express. Available with full prepayment or cash on delivery.
  • Pickup: Not available at the moment. Our farm currently works only as an online shop.

Important: When you choose online payment, the order is packed and shipped only after payment has been confirmed. Payment details and links are available immediately in the success window and in your email. Please remember that under Resolution No. 172 of the Cabinet of Ministers of Ukraine, seeds and sauces of proper quality cannot be returned. Always check product integrity, especially PET bottles, directly at the postal branch. By placing an order, you agree to the Terms of Sale.
